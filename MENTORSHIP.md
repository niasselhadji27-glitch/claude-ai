# Meta Ads Mentorship Roadmap

**Student profile:** Learning media buying as a career · Focus: lead generation for services & local businesses · Practice budget: under $300/month

This is a living document. As we work together, we'll check off milestones and add notes from real campaigns.

---

## How this mentorship works

- We go phase by phase. Each phase has lessons (theory), homework (doing), and a milestone (proof you've got it).
- Ask me anything, anytime: "why is my CPM $40?", "review this ad copy", "is this audience too narrow?" — that's what a mentor is for.
- You will spend real money in Phase 1. Small amounts, deliberately, to learn. Wasted spend on a $150 test is tuition; wasted spend on a client's $3,000/month is a fired media buyer.

---

## Phase 0 — Foundations (Weeks 1–2, $0 spend)

**Goal:** Understand the machine before feeding it money.

### Lessons
1. **How the Meta auction actually works** — you don't pay for ads, you bid for attention. Total value = bid × estimated action rate × ad quality. Why this means creative quality lowers your costs.
2. **Account anatomy** — Business Portfolio → Ad Account → Campaign (objective) → Ad Set (audience, budget, placement) → Ad (creative). What each level controls and the mistakes people make at each.
3. **Tracking** — the Meta Pixel, the Conversions API, and Events Manager. Why lead gen for local businesses often skips this pain entirely with Instant Forms.
4. **The metrics that matter** — CPM, CTR (link), CPC, CPL, frequency, and how to read them as a diagnostic chain, not a scoreboard.

### Homework
- [ ] Create a Meta Business Portfolio and ad account (business.facebook.com)
- [ ] Open Ads Manager and build a full draft campaign — do NOT publish. Just learn every screen.
- [ ] Browse the Meta Ads Library (facebook.com/ads/library) — find 10 ads from local service businesses (dentists, roofers, gyms, cleaners). Save the ones that have been running 60+ days (longevity = they're working).

### Milestone
You can explain to me, in your own words, why two identical businesses can pay wildly different costs per lead.

## Phase 1 — First real campaigns (Weeks 3–6, ~$150–250 total)

**Goal:** Launch, and learn to feel the levers.

### Lessons
1. **The offer comes first** — no targeting or creative saves a weak offer. What makes local offers work: specificity, urgency, low friction ("free quote in 24h" beats "contact us").
2. **Instant Forms vs. landing pages** — for local lead gen at small budgets, Instant Forms (Leads objective) win on cost; landing pages win on lead quality. Start with Instant Forms.
3. **Targeting in the Advantage+ era** — broad targeting + a tight radius + great creative beats interest-stacking for local. The algorithm finds buyers; your creative tells it who to look for.
4. **Creative fundamentals** — the hook (first 3 seconds / first line), the problem, the proof, the CTA. Why ugly, native-looking ads often beat polished ones for local services.
5. **Budgets & the learning phase** — $5–10/day per ad set, why you don't touch anything for 3–4 days, what "Learning Limited" actually means at small budgets (and why it's often fine).

### Homework
- [ ] Pick a practice offer (a real local business you know, your own service, or a realistic dummy offer)
- [ ] Write 3 different hooks for the same offer; I'll review them
- [ ] Launch: 1 campaign, 1 ad set (broad + radius), 3 ads, $7/day, Instant Form
- [ ] Let it run 5 days untouched, then bring me the numbers

### Milestone
A launched campaign and your first cost-per-lead number — good or bad, it's your baseline.

## Phase 2 — Reading data & iterating (Weeks 7–10)

**Goal:** Turn numbers into decisions. This is the actual skill clients pay for.

### Lessons
1. **The diagnostic chain** — high CPM? → auction/creative quality problem. Good CPM, low CTR? → creative problem. Good CTR, no leads? → offer/form problem. Leads but garbage quality? → form friction too low or targeting too loose.
2. **Kill / keep / scale rules** — decision thresholds at small budgets (e.g., no lead at 2× your target CPL = kill), and why you judge ads on spend, not time.
3. **Creative iteration** — you never restart from zero: keep the winning hook, change the format; keep the format, change the hook.
4. **Retargeting on a budget** — engagement audiences (video viewers, form-openers) when you don't have Pixel traffic.

### Rough benchmarks for local lead gen (US/EU, 2025–26 — vary by niche)
| Metric | Healthy range |
|---|---|
| CPM | $15–40 |
| CTR (link) | 1–2%+ |
| Cost per lead (Instant Form) | $5–25 depending on service value |
| Frequency (7-day) | under ~2.5 |

### Milestone
You bring me a losing ad and correctly diagnose *which link in the chain* broke before I tell you.

---

## Phase 3 — Client-ready (Weeks 11+)

**Goal:** Package the skill into a career.

### Lessons
1. **The audit** — how to review a stranger's ad account in 30 minutes and find money leaks (your best sales tool).
2. **Reporting that keeps clients** — clients don't care about CTR; they care about leads, cost per lead, and what you're doing next. One-page weekly reports.
3. **Getting client #1** — the local-business playbook: run ads for one business free or at cost for 60 days in exchange for a case study and testimonial. One documented result beats any certificate.
4. **Pricing** — flat retainer ($500–1,500/month to start) vs. % of ad spend, and why performance-only deals burn beginners.
5. **Onboarding & access** — requesting Business Portfolio access properly (never take logins), what to collect before launch day.

### Milestone
One case study with real numbers, and a repeatable process for the next client.
